I'm Daniel Miranda Danelli, a cloud architect. Most of my work is designing platforms on AWS: Landing Zones, Infrastructure as Code, Governance, Policy as Code and Agentic Solutions. Outside of work I'm usually tinkering with AI, testing new models and agents and figuring out where they actually help. This blog is where I share what I learn along the way.

## Why this site exists

Documentation tells you how things should work. Production tells you how they do. The gap between the two is where most of my time goes, and it's rarely written down. This site is my attempt to close it, in two ways: notes that capture the symptom, why it mattered, how it actually works and the lesson worth keeping, and projects that show the whole thing built end to end.

## What you'll find here

- **Notes.** Lessons from real cloud and AI work. Companies stay unnamed and details are sanitized, but the findings are real.
- **Projects.** Things I've built end to end, at work and at home, with the architecture and decisions behind them.
